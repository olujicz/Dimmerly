# Italian · it

Use informal singular **tu** and direct verbs such as **Riprova** and **Inserisci**, following the existing shortcut and location prompts. Write Latin script with Italian accents and apostrophes. Use «…» in new prose.

Use **schermo** or **display** for the screen and **monitor** when discussing physical controls. Use **luminosità** for brightness, **preimpostazione** for a preset and **scorciatoia** for a shortcut. Input labels and common terms such as **Hardware**, **Software** and **Volume** may match English.

## Decisions

- Address: informal **tu**; omit subject pronouns, avoid redundant possessives where the article suffices.
- Buttons and menu items: second-person imperative (`Riprova`, `Aggiungi`, `Annulla`).
- Gender: no gendered forms address the user; use impersonal or imperative wording. Do not use `*` or `ə`.
- Typography: «…» in new prose, Italian apostrophes and accents.
